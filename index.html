Integrated Port Management System (IPMS)

Problem Statement

A major port authority operated its berth planning, vessel scheduling, and marine services through fragmented manual processes — spreadsheets, phone calls, and paper logs — leading to operational inefficiencies, scheduling conflicts, and compliance gaps.

⚡
No unified visibility Real-time berth occupancy and vessel ETA data existed in isolated silos
🔄
Manual coordination overhead Berth allocations managed via phone and email with frequent conflicts
📋
Compliance documentation gaps Marine clearance and customs documentation lacked digital tracking
📊
No analytics layer Management had no real-time operational dashboards or reporting

Requirement Gathering Process

1
Discovery Workshops

Facilitated structured requirement workshops with port authority management, operations supervisors, marine pilots, and customs officers across 3 site visits.

2
As-Is Process Mapping

Documented current-state workflows using BPMN 2.0 — identified 14 pain points and 8 critical bottlenecks in the existing berth allocation process.

3
BRD / FRD Documentation

Authored a 180-page Business Requirements Document and 240-page Functional Requirements Document covering 12 platform modules with full traceability matrices.

4
User Story Backlog

Created 200+ INVEST-aligned user stories with acceptance criteria, mapped to sprints using MoSCoW prioritization with stakeholder sign-off.

5
Prototyping & Validation

Collaborated with UX team on wireframes; ran 3 rounds of prototype walkthroughs with end users to validate UX flows before development.
